We prove Lemma 2 of the supplied research note. Let $P$ be standard Borel, let $G$ and the groups $A_n$ be Polish groups, and let $h_{n,p}:A_n\to G$ be homomorphisms, jointly Borel in the parameter and group variable and continuous in the group variable. Write
$$
H_p=\langle\bigcup_n h_{n,p}(A_n)\rangle,\qquad M=\{p:z(p)\in H_p\}.
$$
If every coset relation outside $M$ Borel reduces to one fixed Borel equivalence relation, then $M$ is Borel.

Finite products of generator images and their inverses provide analytic witnesses for membership in $H_p$. Hence $M$ is analytic.

For the complementary direction, a fixed Polish code space carries a jointly Borel evaluator representing every Borel map modulo agreement on a comeager set. The evaluator is constructed from countably many basis-coded open tests, least eligible dense target points, and their limit. Borel preimages of a countable family of target balls agree with open sets modulo meager sets, which proves the representation property without choosing reductions Borelly in $p$.

For a candidate code, impose generic invariance under each generator and generic separation under right translation by $z(p)$. Category quantification of Borel sets is Borel. The subgroup of elements preserving the candidate map modulo the target relation on a comeager set is closed under multiplication and inverses, so the generic tests exclude $z(p)\in H_p$.

Conversely, an actual reduction of the coset relation supplies the tests after replacing it by its coded representative. Open shear maps preserve the comeager agreement sets needed for the generator tests. Thus the projection of the Borel set of successful codes is exactly $P\setminus M$, making the complement analytic. Analytic separation now proves $M$ Borel. A compatible Polish topology is chosen on the standard Borel parameter space only to apply the analytic-set machinery; its given measurable structure is retained. Empty targets are handled directly.
